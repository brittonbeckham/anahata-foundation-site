06Get involved

If this calls to you,
reach out.

We are not running a campaign. We are tending a fire and waiting for those who feel its warmth.

Many have felt this land's call. We are here now — living on it, raising our family in it, building what it has always been meant to become. If this vision resonates, we invite you to be part of it. Whatever brings you here, let it begin with a conversation.

01 Contribute
Step into a Founding or Principal Benefactor position to help secure the land into permanent nonprofit stewardship. All contributions are tax-deductible.
02 Introduce
If you know someone who might be aligned — a benefactor, a facilitator, a fellow steward — we would be grateful for the introduction.
03 Reflect
If something here moved you, tell us. Even brief reflections from those who resonate help us understand who is finding their way to the work.
