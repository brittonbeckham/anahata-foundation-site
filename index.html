Anahata (AH–NAH–HA–TAH) is the Sanskrit word for the fourth chakra of the etheric body — the heart center. Its literal meaning is "unstruck," referring to the inner, unstruck sound (Anahata Nāda) that resonates within the heart — the vibration of pure being.

01Welcome

We are Britton and Carolee Beckham. Together with our four children, we are stewards of forty-six acres of mountain land just outside Lyons, Colorado — a place that has been called by many to become a sanctuary, but only now is being prepared to receive its full purpose. We have come to know it as Anahata.

What is forming here is a tri-fold installation: a New Earth Anchor that holds a stable frequency on the land; a Healing Center where the nervous system can settle and the inner world can surface; and a Mystery School where teachings of self, cosmos, and sovereignty are practiced through ritual, embodiment, and direct experience.

This site is an introduction to that vision. The project is real, and it is in motion — yet it is not yet open. We are at the threshold, seeking aligned beneficiaries to help secure this land into permanent stewardship so that its purpose may be served for generations to come.

02The three roles of Anahata

One sanctuary, three intertwined functions — each amplifying the others.

01

New Earth Anchor

A physical and energetic node dedicated to stabilizing and radiating the frequencies of higher human consciousness. Tended through the daily life of a family, the rhythm of ceremony, and the sustained coherence of the land itself.

02

Healing Center

A sanctuary for the multi-dimensional human being — mind, body, and spirit. Here, healing is not a service to be delivered, but a natural outcome of reconnecting to one's essence in a field already attuned to wholeness.

03

Mystery School

A place where personal transformation is taught, practiced, and integrated. Programs in ritual, astrology, embodiment, and shadow work — transmissions that land more deeply because the field has been prepared.
