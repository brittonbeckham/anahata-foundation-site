02 The Project

One sanctuary, three intertwined functions — forged from initiation.

The Vision of the Anahata Foundation is a tri-fold installation: a New Earth Anchor, a Healing Center, and a Mystery School. What follows is the full vision — the land, the philosophy, the structure, and the path that leads from where we stand today to a center capable of serving many thousands from the local and greater community.

01The land

A teacher, alive with all four elements.

Anahata rests on forty-six acres of pristine mountain land roughly four miles north of Lyons, Colorado, just inside Larimer County. The property is composed of a lower meadow of about sixteen acres and an upper meadow of thirty, joined by a dynamic ravine that climbs sixty feet between them. A river runs along the northern edge. A natural circle of large cottonwoods anchors the lower meadow, holding what we have come to recognize as a powerful energy vortex.

The land is beyond the reach of city EMF and cellular signal. The energy feels clean, clear, and ancestral — there is evidence of a Native American camp on the property.

02The residence

Built into the hillside, off-grid by design.

A large residence was built on the land when it was initially purchased in 1997. The design was styled after Santa Fe villas -- tile roof, stucco, and thick 18" exterior adobe walls. The builder followed a sustainability and made the home fully off-grid since inital construction. Running on modern solar and battery technology, there is backup generation already available. The well water here has been effectiviely running for over 30 years with no issues. The thick adobe walls keep the home cool in summer without active cooling, warm in winter through radiant in-floor heat.

Seven bedrooms, five bathrooms, two kitchens, and several living spaces make room for a stewarding family with the space to house visiting guests, overnight retreats, social events, and the intimate facilitation work of small-group gatherings. The home feels earthy and grounded.

03Philosophy

Remembrance over repair.

Anahata's teachings begin from a different premise than most healing work. We hold that there is nothing in any of us that is fundamentally broken — only forgotten, guarded, or asleep. Healing is not the assembly of fixes; it is the radiance of the self causing the conditions in which the self is recognized, by the self.

True healing is simply the radiance of the self causing an environment in which a catalyst may occur which initiates the recognition of self, by self, of the self-healing properties of the self. — Ra, The Law of One

From this premise, the work becomes one of remembrance. We re-member the forgotten parts back together into one great whole. We remember our personal authority over our own lives, releasing victimhood. We remember our spiritual nature, our sovereignty, our unity with creation. The path of healing, the path of awakening, and the path of remembering are one path.

07Five scopes of development

How the vision unfolds, in five movements.

Anahata is being built across five distinct phases or scopes. Each one builds upon the last. The project is seeking the sponsorships for Scopes I & II — the acquisition of the land into permanent nonprofit stewardship and the begging of transforming it into its magical three-fold mission.

  • Three-year operating reserve so Scope II can begin without external pressure

08Where we stand today

At the threshold of Scope I.

Britton and Carolee currently lease the property with the intention to purchase. The owner is eager to sell and supportive of the vision. Our task is to bring the land into permanent stewardship under a Colorado-registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with religious and educational protections, so that title is held by the entity itself — not by any individual — and the land is protected from sale, division, or misuse for generations.

We are seeking aligned stewards. Three Founding Steward positions at $555,000 and one Principal Steward position at $1.11M will secure Scope I in full. All contributions are tax-deductible under 501(c)(3). This is not a traditional investment. It is a contribution to advancing human consciousness — a chance to place capital in the service of something that will outlast all of us.
