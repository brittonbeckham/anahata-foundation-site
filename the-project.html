02 The Project

One sanctuary, three intertwined functions — forged from initiation.

The Vision of the Anahata Foundation is a tri-fold installation: a New Earth Anchor, a Healing Center, and a Mystery School. What follows is the full vision — the land, the philosophy, the structure, and the path that leads from where we stand today to a center capable of serving tens of thousands.

01The land

A teacher, alive with all four elements.

Anahata rests on forty-six acres of pristine mountain land roughly five miles north of Lyons, Colorado, just inside Larimer County. The property is composed of a lower meadow of about sixteen acres and an upper meadow of thirty, joined by a dynamic ravine that climbs sixty feet between them. A river runs along the northern edge. A natural circle of large cottonwoods anchors the lower meadow, holding what we have come to recognize as a powerful energy vortex.

The land is beyond the reach of city EMF and cellular signal. The energy here feels clean, clear, and ancestral — there is evidence of a Native American camp on the property. The residence is built into the hillside, fully off-grid, running on solar and battery with backup generation, drawing water from a well into an eighteen-hundred-gallon cistern. Eighteen-inch adobe walls keep the home cool in summer without active cooling, warm in winter through radiant in-floor heat.

04Philosophy

Remembrance over repair.

Anahata's teachings begin from a different premise than most healing work. We hold that there is nothing in any of us that is fundamentally broken — only forgotten, guarded, or asleep. Healing is not the assembly of fixes; it is the radiance of the self causing the conditions in which the self is recognized, by the self.

True healing is simply the radiance of the self causing an environment in which a catalyst may occur which initiates the recognition of self, by self, of the self-healing properties of the self. — Ra, The Law of One

From this premise, the work becomes one of remembrance. We re-member the forgotten parts back together into one great whole. We remember our personal authority over our own lives, releasing victimhood. We remember our spiritual nature, our sovereignty, our unity with creation. The path of healing, the path of awakening, and the path of remembering are one path.

05The four pillars

The structure beneath everything taught here.

Four living principles, each a bridge between the inner and outer worlds. They are not abstract ideals — they are the practical pathways through which remembrance occurs.

01

Magick

The art of conscious creation — aligning will, imagination, and emotion with universal law. Spelled with a k, after the older traditions, to distinguish the sacred art from stage illusion.

02

Ritual

Highly intentional, aligned action. The bridge between physical and spiritual — the architecture that holds magick in place and reminds us that any moment can be made holy through presence.

03

Play

The frequency of creation. Curiosity, imagination, and lightness as a path — because laughter often carries us deeper into truth than effort and striving ever could.

04

Astrology

The Cosmic Clock — the language of divine timing. Planetary movement informs the rhythm of our rituals, the design of our teachings, and the pacing of the center's growth. As above, so below.

07Where we stand today

At the threshold of Scope I.

Britton and Carolee currently lease the property with the intention to purchase. The owner is eager to sell and supportive of the vision. Our task is to bring the land into permanent stewardship under a Colorado-registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit with religious and educational protections, so that title is held by the entity itself — not by any individual — and the land is protected from sale, division, or misuse for generations.

We are seeking aligned stewards. Three Founding Steward positions at $555,000 and one Principal Steward position at $1.11M will secure Scope I in full. All contributions are tax-deductible under 501(c)(3). This is not a traditional investment. It is a contribution to advancing human consciousness — a chance to place capital in the service of something that will outlast all of us.
