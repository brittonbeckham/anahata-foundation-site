The Path

Forged, not aspired.

The story of Anahata begins long before we arrived in Colorado. In 2017, our relationship reached a breaking point that made it unmistakably clear that surface change would be insufficient. We committed instead to disciplined work — individually, and together. In 2019, a profound ceremonial healing experience clarified the depth of what we were being asked to undertake, and we began the long, intentional process of walking a sovereign, embodied spiritual path.

In the years that followed, each of us was guided into complementary modalities. Carolee completed a year-long Integrative Healing Facilitator training. Britton entered formal study and practice in shamanic sound healing. These were not exploratory interests. They were sustained initiations.

In 2022 we sold nearly all of our belongings and embarked on what became an eighteen-month soul-led pilgrimage with our four children — through Bali, France, Ireland, England, Scotland, and Egypt. It was while in Egypt that a shared knowing emerged: the work we were being prepared for would root in Colorado. When we arrived in early 2024, the resonance was immediate. The land we now call Anahata was not a blank canvas — it was a place we had been prepared to steward.

Wisdom is not accumulated. It is forged.

Britton

Architect of vision and structure.

Father, visionary teacher, shamanic sound healer. After a twenty-two-year career in software engineering, Britton followed an inner calling to step beyond the 9-to-5 world and dedicate his life to healing and transformation. His work bridges the practical and the mystical — integrating emotional intelligence, energy work, and embodied spiritual practice into a grounded, accessible approach to growth.

Raised in a humble home, he discovered an early passion for computers and became a self-taught technologist. Decades of corporate and entrepreneurial work cultivated a rare balance of vision and discipline — a creativity tempered by pragmatism, now informing his approach to conscious creation.

At Anahata, Britton serves as the architect of vision and structure, grounding the project through clarity, focus, and unwavering connection to purpose.

Carolee

Embodiment of the divine feminine.

Mother, astrologer, artist, teacher, embodiment mentor. Carolee embodies the role of a modern priestess through her dedication to the Divine Feminine. Her journey through betrayal trauma into a life of self-love, sovereignty, and emotional liberation gives her work depth and authenticity. She guides others to meet themselves with compassion, to reclaim their bodies and voices, and to live with heart-centered focus.

What also defines Carolee is her devotion. She doesn't simply study the feminine — she embodies it as a living practice, tending to her family, her rituals, and the spaces she holds with reverence. Through her passion for astrology and her honoring of the Celtic Wheel of the Year, she naturally bridges the celestial and the earthly, helping others root cosmic wisdom into lived experience.

Her wisdom carries the imprint of initiation. She has passed through rupture, descent, and reclamation, alchemizing pain into clarity and inner authority.

"Anahata emerged from our shared awakening. It stands as a reflection of our devotion to the evolution of consciousness, and our belief that healing the self heals the world." — Britton & Carolee, raising four children at Anahata
